01 — The Beginning

Not a freelancer.
A founder.

I graduated with my Master's in Human-Computer Interaction and asked a question most new grads don't: What if I just built the thing myself?

I looked around Syracuse and saw small businesses — restaurants, salons, liquor stores — struggling with bad design, zero social media, and no digital presence. Great products, invisible online.

I founded Ukraft Designs — "You + Craft" — and started pitching. I didn't chase volume. I built deep, long-term relationships with 5 core clients, each trusting me with their entire digital identity, month after month.

01
The Restaurant — Where It All Started
Food & Beverage · Syracuse, NY
Anchor Client

A newly founded restaurant in Syracuse — great food, zero digital presence. No website, no social media, no posters. They needed everything built from scratch, designed to work for everyone from elderly regulars to young families.

I pitched them a full solution. They were impressed. They said yes. That was the beginning of everything.

I started fast — posters and menus first, tangible results within 48 hours. Then the website, designed with accessibility at the core. Then social media management. Month after month, the work compounded — and so did their trust. They put me on a monthly retainer.

Then something remarkable happened. The restaurant grew — a second location, then a food truck. Because they already trusted me, I was their designer for every new expansion. One client became three separate full design projects.

02
The Liquor Store
Retail · Website Redesign + Video
Redesign + Video

They already had a website — but it wasn't working. They came with a vague sense of what needed to change. Rather than guessing, I slowed down, listened carefully, and created rough drafts based entirely on what I heard.

When I presented those drafts, they saw their own vision reflected back at them. That's what discovery-first design does. We iterated from there together until the site felt exactly right.

Then they asked if I could handle video too — content creation, editing, social media. I said yes. I delivered. The relationship expanded naturally because the trust was already built.

04
The Security Company
B2B Services · Professional Web Presence
B2B Design

A security company is the opposite of a restaurant. The brand needed to project authority, trust, and credibility above everything else. I had to completely shift my visual language — and that's exactly what made this project exciting.

Every design decision was driven by B2B conversion thinking: who visits this site, what makes them feel confident, and how do you get them to take action. Credibility signals, service clarity, and professional presence drove the entire design.

04 — How I Worked

A process that
actually scaled.

01
Discovery First
Stakeholder interviews, brand audit, competitor scan. Understand before designing — always.
02
Show in 48 Hours
Tangible drafts fast. Build trust through speed and responsiveness, not promises.
03
Build Together
Iterate with the client. Their input shapes every decision — that's what makes it feel like theirs.
04
Measure & Stay
Track views, engagement, business impact. Use data to keep improving. That's why clients never left.

05 — What I Learned

The education
money can't buy.

Business
Pricing is positioning
How you charge signals your quality before a client sees a single pixel. I learned to price for value delivered, not hours logged.
Operations
Scope clarity prevents burnout
A vague brief is a bottomless pit. Every project starts with a written scope — what's in, what's out, what success looks like.
Design
Simplicity wins every time
Small businesses need clarity, not cleverness. The best work made their life easier — not work that impressed other designers.
Product Thinking
Every business is a UX problem
A menu redesign, a posting schedule, a website flow — they're all user experience problems. HCI training applied to the real world.
Client Work
Listen harder than you design
The liquor store redesign worked because I listened first. The draft reflected what they actually said — not what I assumed they wanted.
Growth
Depth beats breadth
I didn't get 50 clients. I got 5 great clients and did 50 projects for them. That's how you build a studio worth being proud of.
